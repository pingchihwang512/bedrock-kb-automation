## Daily Itinerary

### DAY 1：USA- Cairo, Egypt / 美國 - 開羅，埃及

- **[English]**: Board an international flight bound for Cairo, Egypt’s capital and Africa’s largest metropolis.
- **[中文]**: 搭機飛往北非的第一大城、埃及的首都－開羅，展開埃及開羅與尼羅河探秘之旅。

### DAY 2：Cairo / 開羅

- **Hotel**: Sofitel Cairo Downtown Nile or Similar (Standard Twin)
- **[English]**: Arrive at Cairo International Airport and meet your tour representative in the arrivals hall. From there, enjoy a comfortable transfer to your deluxe hotel in the heart of this vast and fast-growing city. Straddling the River Nile, Africa’s largest metropolis combines ancient monuments, vibrant bazaars, and modern urban life, all wrapped in a whirl of color and energy that travelers find intoxicating. After checking in at your hotel, take some time to relax and prepare for tomorrow’s sightseeing.
- **[中文]**: 抵達開羅機場，當地導遊接機後，專車送往位於尼羅河畔的豪華酒店，休息宿夜。埃及，五千年悠久的歷史，是世界四大文明古國之一，首都開羅座落在富庶的尼羅河三角洲頂端，是世界最古老的城巿之一，被譽為「城巿之母」，也是阿拉伯國家政治活動的中心，古跡遍佈，是許多人一生夢想一遊之地。

### DAY 3：Cairo / 開羅

- **Hotel**: Sofitel Cairo Downtown Nile or Similar (Standard Twin)
- **Meals**: Breakfast at hotel, Lunch, Dinner
- **[English]**: Enjoy a hotel breakfast and spend the day discovering the star attractions of this extraordinary city, whose origins date back to 2,000 B.C. Begin with a visit to the Giza Plateau, on Cairo’s western edge, to view its UNESCO-listed Great Pyramids. Marvel at these vast pharaohs’ tombs, built around 4,500 years ago, and the neighboring Sphinx as your guide shares their secrets and stories. Next, stop by a Papyrus Factory for an educational visit. Then continue to the Grand Egyptian Museum to see the treasures of Tutankhamun and other artifacts, including ancient animal mummies, household goods, and more. After exploring the incredible displays, travel on to the 14th-century Khan el Khalili Bazaar, Cairo’s largest souk, to wander its maze of stall-filled lanes. Later, enjoy dinner at a local restaurant before returning to your hotel for overnight.
- **[中文]**: 酒店內早餐後，展開埃及古文明之旅。首先，參觀【吉薩金字塔群】。有人說，騎駱駝觀金字塔是一生難得的體驗，有機會可以試試，費用自理。金字塔，是埃及的象徵，建於公元前2500多年，是世界七大奇景之一，神秘的【人面獅身像】靜臥塔前。幾千年來，雖然時光流逝，人事全非，人面獅身像卻仍在這浩瀚的沙漠中，默默守護著金字塔。返回巿區，途中造訪【莎草紙廠】，瞭解古老莎草紙的製作過程；再到【大埃及博物館】，館內收藏豐富的文物，其中，法老王圖坦卡門的黃金面具及陪葬品最具傳奇性，木乃伊是館內另一個熱門展示。隨後，閒逛號稱中東最大、起源於14世紀的【卡利利傳統巿集】，選購各種金飾及特產香精油，或品嚐埃及奶茶，感受當地的氛圍。當地餐廳享用晚餐後，返回酒店。

### DAY 4：Cairo – Luxor – Nile Cruise / 開羅 – 洛克索 – 尼羅河遊輪

- **Hotel**: Movenpick Nile Cruise or Similar (Standard Room)
- **Meals**: Breakfast at hotel, Lunch, Dinner
- **[English]**: This morning, transfer to the airport for your flight to Luxor. Upon arrival, begin your tour with a visit to the ancient relics of the Karnak Complex on the Nile’s East Bank, wandering among its massive pillars and sanctuaries. Later, board the 5-star cruise ship on the Nile—Egypt’s lifeblood for thousands of years. Enjoy lunch in the elegant dining room before joining your guide for an afternoon of sightseeing. Stroll through the halls and courts of Luxor Temple as your guide reveals the stories of the pharaohs who built them. Return to the ship for dinner and overnight.
- **[中文]**: 今日，搭機赴古埃及的首都、舊稱底比斯的旅遊重鎮－洛克索。洛克索的古蹟處處，散佈在尼羅河的東西兩岸，規模浩大的【卡納克神廟群】，神廟的大柱廳裡，密集的巨柱群特別撼動人心。遊畢，登上五星級的豪華遊輪享用午餐，展開尼羅河遊輪之旅。湍流水急的尼羅河，是埃及的文化搖籃、生命之河，也是世界第二長河，由南向北流經撒哈拉沙漠，注入地中海，將埃及分為東西兩岸，孕育了五千年黃金歲月的文明。下午，下船，隨著導遊參觀世界最大的露天史前古蹟－【洛克索神廟】，成排巨大的雕像、聳立的廊柱、巍然的方尖碑、崩塌的殿宇，讓人深深感受到古埃及帝國的強盛與輝煌。遊輪夜泊洛克索。

### DAY 5：Nile Cruise – Luxor – Esna – Edfu / 尼羅河遊輪 – 洛克索 – 伊斯納 – 艾德芙

- **Hotel**: Movenpick Nile Cruise or Similar (Standard Room)
- **Meals**: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
- **[English]**: After breakfast, admire the two massive statues of the Colossi of Memnon and visit a selection of pharaohs’ tombs at the Valley of the Kings and the Temple of Hatshepsut. Then, return to your ship to sail to Edfu via the Esna Lock. Enjoy lunch on board and take in the Nile scenery as you cruise upstream to Edfu, where you will dock for the night.
- **[中文]**: 早餐後，先遊西岸的谷地。這裡，法老王阿曼哈德三世曾依山而建一座神廟，坍塌後，只剩兩座高聳的【門農石像】，至今仍然氣宇非凡。【帝王谷】，是一片遍佈著多位古埃及法老王陵墓的山谷，大部份陵墓中的陪葬品早被偷盜一空，或被移往開羅博物館存放，現今看到的，主要是宏偉壯觀的墓室及栩栩如生的壁畫。【哈特謝普蘇特神廟】，哈特謝普蘇特是第一個名垂千古的古埃及女性法老，也是古埃及法老時代唯一一個統治過埃及的女性，神殿三層樓設計方式，開闊簡潔，很有現代建築的風格。登船享用午餐，穿梭於各大古蹟之中時間緩緩流逝，在寂靜的夜色中，遊輪緩緩駛過伊斯納水閘，晚間抵達艾德芙。

### DAY 6：Nile Cruise – Edfu – Kom Ombo – Aswan / 尼羅河遊輪 – 艾德芙 – 康蒙波 – 亞斯旺

- **Hotel**: Movenpick Nile Cruise or Similar (Standard Room)
- **Meals**: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
- **[English]**: Begin your tour of Edfu by taking a horse-drawn carriage to its highlight—the Temple of Edfu, dedicated to the falcon-headed god Horus. Dwarfed in size only by Karnak, this well-preserved complex unfolds through a series of courtyards and halls lined with statues, reliefs, and pillars. After exploring, enjoy lunch on board and take in the views from the deck as you sail to Kom Ombo. Upon arrival, visit the Kom Ombo Temple, built between 108 and 47 B.C., notable for its unique double design: two parallel temples dedicated to the god Horus and Sobek, the crocodile god. After your visit, rejoin the ship and continue cruising to the ancient city of Aswan.
- **[中文]**: 尼羅河沿岸遍佈著數不清的古埃及遺跡，俯拾皆是。早餐後，下船，搭乘馬車前往供奉著鷹神荷魯斯的【艾德芙神廟】，這個埃及第二大神廟，規模僅次於卡納克神廟群，保存完整。返回遊輪享用午餐，下午遊輪抵達康蒙波，下船參觀【康蒙波神廟】。康蒙波神廟是由兩間神廟所組成，供奉著老鷹之神和鰐魚之神，有兩個入口、廊柱大廳和聖殿，矗立在尼羅河畔的一處河灣岩層上，在古代，曾是鰐魚聚集之處，神廟證明了古埃及的祭司對尼羅河的自然定律以及尼羅河鰐魚的敬重。夜色中，遊輪續行，抵達亞斯旺。

### DAY 7：Nile Cruise – Aswan – Optional Tour to Abu Simbel – Aswan / 尼羅河遊輪 – 亞斯旺 – 阿布辛貝大神廟（自費）– 亞斯旺

- **Hotel**: Movenpick Nile Cruise or Similar (Standard Room)
- **Meals**: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
- **Optional Tour**: Abu Simbel Excursion (by Bus) / 阿布辛貝大神廟 ($250/pp)
- **[English]**: This morning is free at leisure. You may choose to join the optional tour to Abu Simbel, home to the Great Temple of Ramses II on the banks of Lake Nasser. Admire the temple’s enormous Ramses II statues and explore its interiors while learning how it was rescued from the rising Nile during the late 20th century. After lunch on board, soak in the beauty of the Nile as a wooden 'Felucca' sailboat ferries you around the Aga Khan Mausoleum. Return to the ship for dinner and overnight at Aswan.
- **[中文]**: 亞斯旺的氣候溫和，風景優美，是著名的旅遊勝地，也是去阿布辛貝和非洲的必經之地。今天上午自由活動，有興趣，可以參加自費活動《阿布辛貝大神廟》，前往位於埃及最南部、鄰近蘇丹的阿布辛貝，欣賞由一整座岩山鬼斧神工雕鑿而成的大神廟，廟前四尊拉美西斯二世的巨大石像，廟內牆壁刻滿壁畫，炫耀其一生輝煌政績。神廟原面向尼羅河，亞斯旺水壩興建後水位升高，因而搬往高地，改而面向水壩，導遊將解說一段世界近史上偉大的遷建史。返回遊輪享用午餐，下午，體驗浪漫的【三桅帆船之旅】，乘坐尼羅河特有的三桅帆船，幻想自己成為不可一世的埃及艷后，悠遊徜徉於尼羅河上，遠眺與世隔絕的【阿嘎可汗王陵】，風格典雅的粉紅色花崗岩建築，令人無限嚮往。

### DAY 8：Nile Cruise – Aswan – Cairo / 尼羅河遊輪 – 亞斯旺 – 開羅

- **Hotel**: Sofitel Cairo Downtown Nile or Similar (Standard Twin)
- **Meals**: Breakfast, Dinner
- **[English]**: Disembark your ship this morning and explore Aswan’s highlights, including the High Dam and the Temple of Philae—one of Nubia’s most important monuments, saved from a watery grave by UNESCO’s rescue project during the construction of the Aswan High Dam. Later, board a flight to Cairo for your final two nights in the city.
- **[中文]**: 早餐後，下船，結束尼羅河遊輪之旅，展開亞斯旺巿區遊覽。參觀【高壩】，高壩是為徹底解決尼羅河氾濫的問題所興建，用掉的石塊約吉薩大金字塔的18倍，工程之艱鉅可想而知！美麗的【菲萊神廟】，原建於菲萊島上，同樣也因尼羅河水泛濫的問題，在國際資助下費時二十年才搬遷到現今小島上。遊畢，告別亞斯旺，搭機返回開羅。

### DAY 9：Cairo - Optional Tour to Saqqara - Citadel - Hanging Church - Cairo / 開羅一日遊 - 薩卡拉 - 薩拉丁城堡 - 懸空教堂 (自費) - 開羅

- **Hotel**: Sofitel Cairo Downtown Nile or Similar (Standard Twin)
- **Meals**: Breakfast at hotel, Dinner
- **Optional Tour**: Cairo - Sakkara - Citadel - Hanging Church / 開羅一日遊 - 薩卡拉 - 薩拉丁城堡 - 懸空教堂 ($150/pp)
- **[English]**: Today is yours to enjoy as you please, either taking free time to sightsee and stroll or joining our optional Cairo day tour to Memphis, Saqqara, and the Djoser Pyramid. You will visit Memphis, the first capital of ancient Egypt (circa 3100 B.C.), and then continue to Saqqara to see the Djoser Pyramid, also known as the Step Pyramid, the oldest pyramid in Egypt. Next, visit the Hanging Church, the most famous Coptic Christian church in Cairo, and the medieval Islamic fortification, the Cairo Citadel. Built in the 12th century, the Citadel sits on the Muqattam Hills to protect against invading Crusaders and offers a panoramic view of Cairo. Tonight, enjoy a farewell dinner at a local restaurant.
- **[中文]**: 全日自由活動，可自行遊覽開羅，拜訪穆罕默德阿里清真寺，逛逛十二世紀時為對抗十字軍東征而建的薩拉丁城堡。或者，參加自費活動《開羅一日遊》前往孟菲斯薩卡拉和佐瑟金字塔。參觀建於公元前 3100 年古埃及的第一個首都孟菲斯市，參觀薩卡拉金字塔群中的左賽爾金字塔，也被稱為階梯金字塔，是埃及最古老的金字塔。隨後參觀開羅最著名的科普特基督教教堂 （懸空教堂），以及中世紀的伊斯蘭防禦工事——薩拉丁城堡 （也叫開羅城堡）。城堡建於 12 世紀，位於開羅東郊的穆蓋塔姆山上。城堡高高在上，是11世紀薩拉丁將軍為抵禦入侵而建造的。寺外的亭子可俯瞰開羅市的全景。今晚，參加惜別晚宴，與團友們歡聚一堂，度過一個愉快的夜晚。
